# Source notation prepared for the v5 agreement

Prepared 13 October 2026. These are source-reading artifacts, not model outputs or main research data.

- `fig033-note-events.csv` and the two `strube-fig033-*.musicxml` files transcribe the first panel labelled good and the panel labelled avoid on Strube 1928 p. 13, PDF p. 19. There are 16 note events in total. The second good panel is not included.
- `fig033-transcription-provenance.json` records pitches, durations, image/source hashes, stem-to-voice interpretation, the editorial meter, and verification scope. Source accuracy is assistant-checked; independent researcher confirmation is pending.
- `chorale-source-audit.json` inventories all five SATB systems of MODEL 285 and the adjacent exercise notation, including limitations. It is not a complete event transcription.
- `model285-opening-soprano.csv` and its provenance remain the previous partial nine-note reading. No whole-score transcription is claimed. Raw Audiveris fragments are rejected and stay in ignored output folders.

Source images remain under ignored `references/ocr/`. Local round-trip and detector results are in `research/outputs/transcriptions/strube-rule-examples/verification.json`. Detection agrees with two printed overlap labels; this does not validate the whole instrument.

The design rationale and source map are in `docs/final-thesis/study/peta-kaidah-gerak-suara-strube.md`; the current agreement is `docs/final-thesis/supervision/rancangan-kesepakatan-v5.md`.
