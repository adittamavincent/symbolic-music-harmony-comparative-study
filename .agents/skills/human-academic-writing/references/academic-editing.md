# Paragraph workflow for this thesis

This workflow is a local editorial adaptation. Its paragraph checks are not StoryScope features or a validated writing instrument.

## Establish a paragraph's job

For a difficult paragraph or new text, note internally:

| Question | What to record |
| --- | --- |
| What does the reader need here? | A definition, supporting evidence, comparison, method decision, interpretation, limitation, or transition. |
| What can this paragraph actually say? | The supported proposition, with its population, model, context, and claim strength. |
| What supports it? | Existing passage, source page, protocol decision, or measured artifact. Distinguish these origins. |
| What is still unknown? | Only consequential gaps, disagreement, or uncertainty actually present in the material. |
| Why is it here? | What it adds to the preceding argument and what the next paragraph needs from it. |

Use this map only when it helps. A simple grammar fix does not need a formal ledger. A new section benefits from a compact claim/source map; preserve it in task notes or an existing project record, not in thesis prose.

Do not translate the map into five prescribed sentences. A definition may need two sentences; a methodological comparison may need a longer paragraph. Paragraph length and syntax follow the substance.

## When agents write one paragraph at a time

An isolated paragraph can sound polished and still damage the chapter. Before writing, read the full section, its purpose within the chapter, and neighboring paragraphs. After writing, reread the joined passage.

- Carry forward terms already defined; do not define AI, MIDI, or a model anew every time it appears.
- Use the project's term rather than introducing a near-synonym that might imply a new construct.
- Keep the distinction between source findings, researcher design, and this study's measured findings visible where it is needed.
- Keep the scope of the research questions and the unit of analysis consistent across chapters.
- Do not end every paragraph with “Dengan demikian, penelitian ini ...”. End on its last useful claim or connect to the actual next issue.
- Remove a summary only if it repeats. Keep a transition that explains a real change of topic, scale, or inference.
- If work is already divided among agents, give each writer the neighboring text, source material, agreed terms, and evidence boundaries. The integrating editor reads the assembled section. This does not authorize spawning agents or another editing task by itself.

## Indonesian grammar and cadence

These are language choices, not results measured by StoryScope.

- Keep a clear subject and predicate. Repair dangling “yang,” detached subordinate clauses, and unclear “hal ini,” “tersebut,” or “itu” when the referent is ambiguous.
- Split a sentence when several distinct claims make its syntax or citation scope hard to follow. Retain a longer sentence when the relation is clear. Do not apply a fixed word limit or alternate lengths mechanically.
- Prefer a direct verb where it retains meaning: “melakukan penghitungan” can become “menghitung.” Keep established technical nouns when replacing them changes the construct.
- Prefer a concrete actor when responsibility matters: “peneliti menetapkan” for a researcher decision, “Strube menyatakan” for a source statement, or “program menghitung” for implementation. Useful passives such as “melodi dipilih secara acak” remain acceptable.
- Use conjunctions for the real relation. “Karena itu” needs a reason; “namun” needs a contrast; “selain itu” needs an addition. Remove a connector that announces no relation.
- Use parallel syntax in lists. Distinguish `di` as a preposition from the passive prefix `di-`, and `ke` as a preposition from a prefix. Preserve quotations and code literally.
- Use the manuscript's established register and citation convention. Do not add “aku,” “kamu,” anecdotes, rhetorical questions, or casual expressions to make academic prose seem human.
- Keep legitimate repetition of a term. `Pelanggaran`, `kesalahan musikal`, and `ketidakpatuhan` are not automatically equivalent; `hasil`, `keluaran`, and `data` can name different things.
- Keep uncertainty with a function. “Dapat mencerminkan” can mark a real inferential limit; “sangat penting” often adds no evidence. Concision must not increase certainty.
- Do not translate every technical term into a novel Indonesian phrase. Explain an unfamiliar term once when useful, then use it consistently.

## Separate explanation from commentary

Keep explanation that lets the reader reconstruct the method or assess an inference: what is counted, which voices are compared, how time is represented, what the denominator is, and which exceptions are omitted.

Cut commentary that merely tells the reader how to value the paragraph: “Temuan ini sangat penting,” “Hal ini menunjukkan betapa kompleksnya ...,” or “Pendekatan ini memberikan kontribusi signifikan” without a concrete supported consequence.

Do not delete a limitation because it disrupts a neat progression. Conversely, do not add a counterargument to every paragraph; include one when the claim needs it.

## Examples and counterexamples

The examples below are teaching fixtures, not edits to the current manuscript or new research results. Facts are available only within each stated fixture. Cite verified project sources when applying the lesson to real text.

### 1. Remove a significance label

Before:

> Program menghitung pelanggaran per birama. Hal ini menunjukkan pentingnya pendekatan yang sistematis dan komprehensif dalam mengevaluasi musik buatan AI.

After:

> Program menghitung pelanggaran per birama.

The second sentence adds no mechanism, evidence, or limitation. Do not replace it with another significance claim.

### 2. Keep necessary explanation

Before:

> Pelanggaran per kesempatan adalah jumlah pelanggaran dibagi jumlah kesempatan yang memenuhi syarat untuk kaidah tersebut. Penyebut ini berbeda dari jumlah birama.

After: retain the passage if the denominator distinction is needed here.

An edit to “Pelanggaran dihitung secara proporsional” loses the operational definition. Explicitness is useful in this passage.

### 3. Make attribution specific only when supported

Fixture evidence: an existing verified note says Huang et al. (2019), page 798, counted parallel fifths and octaves in Coconet outputs.

Before:

> Beberapa penelitian menunjukkan bahwa keluaran AI memiliki persoalan gerak suara.

After:

> Huang et al. (2019, hlm. 798) menghitung kuint dan oktaf sejajar pada harmonisasi Coconet.

The revision makes the available observation specific and narrows its scope. Without that evidence, flag the missing attribution instead of inventing it. Do not attach this source to a wider claim about all AI music.

### 4. Preserve a real causal limit

Before:

> DeepBach dan Coconet berbeda data latih, representasi, dan prosedur generasi. Karena itu, perbedaan keluarannya tidak dapat dikaitkan dengan arsitektur saja.

After: retain the passage when these differences and the limit are supported.

“Perbedaan arsitektur menentukan mutu harmonisasi” would add a causal claim and change the outcome. “Keduanya mungkin berbeda” would discard the specific reasons.

### 5. Preserve plan versus observation

Fixture: the protocol specifies 40 melodies and five generations per model; main generation has not run.

Before:

> Sebanyak 40 melodi akan diharmonisasi lima kali oleh setiap model.

After: retain the future meaning, even if the syntax is otherwise edited.

“Kedua model menghasilkan harmonisasi untuk 40 melodi” incorrectly reports completed generation. A number in the protocol is not a measured sample count.

### 6. Fix syntax without inventing meaning

Before:

> Dengan menggunakan melodi yang sama. Perbandingan dilakukan pada kedua model.

After:

> Kedua model dibandingkan pada melodi yang sama.

The dangling fragment becomes a complete sentence. Do not append an unsupported promise that all other conditions are identical.

### 7. Leave a clear sentence alone

Before:

> Harmonisasi Bach dipakai sebagai acuan karena Bach sendiri tidak selalu mengikuti kaidah buku ajar.

After: retain if the source supports it and the context makes the reference clear.

No variation quota requires replacing `acuan` with `tolok ukur`, `standar ideal`, or `pembanding objektif`; the latter two also change meaning.

### 8. Detect mode preserves the text

Input:

> Temuan ini menjadi tonggak penting bagi pemahaman musik AI dan membuka cakrawala baru bagi penelitian berikutnya.

Audit output:

> **Importance puffery**: “menjadi tonggak penting ... membuka cakrawala baru.” Replace with the specific supported finding or remove the sentence.

Give the location in a real audit. Do not return an unsolicited rewrite, guess authorship, or assign a human percentage.

## Preserve markup and evidence

Compare before and after for changed numbers, negations, modals, denominators, subject populations, and causal language. A revision can read better while being scientifically worse.

Keep `\parencite[798]{huang2019}`, `\parencites`, citation page ranges, `\label`, `\ref`, math, and technical formatting intact unless the task requires a supported correction. Check the scope of prose around a preserved citation too; keeping a citation token alone does not preserve what it supports.

For file edits, inspect the diff and run `git diff --check`. Build using the existing project workflow when the changes affect LaTeX commands, references, or layout materially. Skill documentation or plain-language examples alone do not require model runs or a thesis build.
