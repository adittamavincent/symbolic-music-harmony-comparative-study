# Writing checks

Derived from the no-AI-slop instructions supplied by the researcher. No separate `eval.md` was present when v3 preparation began.

- The text answers a specific question for its intended reader.
- The edit preserves meaning, vocabulary, and useful personal cadence.
- Each claim has a source, a measured artifact, or an explicit provisional status.
- Planned work is not described as completed work.
- Numbers identify their source, sample, denominator, and limitations.
- No invented lecturer comments, approvals, citations, or results appear.
- No filler, dramatic reveals, unsupported novelty, or decorative emphasis remains.
- Sentence and paragraph shapes follow the content instead of a repeated formula.
- Technical terms help the reader understand the method and are used consistently.
- Conclusions answer the research questions within the actual evidence.
- A change summary explains substantive edits and any reorganization.
